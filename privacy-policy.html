Privacy Policy

Effective date: 06 September 2026

Samara Assisted Living (“Samara”, “we”, “our” or “us”) respects the privacy of residents, family members, applicants, visitors, employees and other persons who communicate with us. This policy explains how personal information is collected, used, stored and protected when you use our website, Family Portal, staff ERP, WhatsApp communications and related digital services.

1. Information we may collect

  • Identity and contact details such as name, mobile number, email address and address.
  • Resident admission, care, clinical, medication, billing and service information where required for assisted-living operations.
  • Family/authorised-contact details and Family Portal access information.
  • Employment or applicant information submitted through recruitment forms.
  • Messages, enquiries and interaction records sent through WhatsApp, website forms, email or other communication channels.
  • Technical information needed to keep our digital services secure and reliable.

2. How we use information

We use personal information only for legitimate operational purposes including resident care and administration, admissions, family communication, billing, service delivery, recruitment, responding to enquiries, security, audit, legal compliance and improving our services.

3. WhatsApp communications

Samara may use the WhatsApp Business Platform/Cloud API to send or receive service messages, authorised family communications, patient-care reports, payment or package notifications, appointment or admission information, emergency or urgent-contact messages, and replies to enquiries. Messages are handled through Meta/WhatsApp systems and may also be recorded in Samara's authorised internal systems for continuity, audit and follow-up.

Where required by WhatsApp rules, Samara uses approved message templates and respects applicable messaging windows and user choices.

4. Family Portal and ERP

The Family Portal provides authorised relatives or contacts with access to information made available for the relevant resident. The staff ERP is restricted to authorised personnel according to assigned roles. Access credentials must not be shared.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary with authorised Samara personnel, care/service providers, technology and communication providers, payment or hosting providers, professional advisers, or government/regulatory authorities where required by law.

6. Data security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information. Records are retained only for as long as reasonably required for care, operational, contractual, audit, legal or regulatory purposes, after which they may be securely deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact Samara to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to legal, clinical, contractual and record-retention obligations. For deletion requests, please see our Data Deletion Instructions.

8. Third-party services and links

Our services may use or link to third-party platforms such as WhatsApp/Meta, cloud hosting, maps or payment services. Their own privacy terms may also apply when you use those services.

9. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, legal obligations or technology change. The latest version will be published on this page with the effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact care@samaraassistedliving.com.
